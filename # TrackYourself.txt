# TrackYourself
**Plan your PATH: Prayers • Attendance • Tasks • Habits**

One simple Android app for the things a student does every day. Free, offline, no ads, no account.

## Features
- **Attendance:** subject cards with your percentage, how many classes you can still skip, and a calendar for each subject
- **Habits:** daily streaks, a weekly view and ready-made habits
- **Tasks:** one-time and repeating tasks
- **Prayers:** offline salah reminders (Karachi method, Hanafi Asr)
- Backup and restore, dark theme, accent colours, app lock

## Privacy
Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Install
1. Go to **Releases** and download the latest `TrackYourself-xx.apk`.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose **Install anyway**. The app is new and isn't on the Play Store yet.
4. To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Your data stays.
